1. RESEARCH QUESTION & EXPERIMENTAL SETUP
  - Research Question: Does an ensemble of complementary ML classifiers improve
    the performance and reliability of multi-domain document classification
    compared with individual classifiers?
  - Baseline Reference: Standalone LinearSVC (C=0.5)
  - Ensembles Tested: Hard Voting (NB+LR+SVC) and Stacking (NB+LR+SVC -> LR Meta-Learner)
  - Split: 18,060 Train / 3,870 Validation / 3,870 Test
  - Leakage Safeguards: 5-Fold Stratified OOF for stacking meta-learner; TF-IDF fit strictly on Train;
    Test split evaluated exactly once.

7. SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
  - In this 6-domain document classification benchmark, the standalone LinearSVC
    classifier demonstrated the strongest generalization on the unseen test set.
  - The added complexity of Hard Voting and Stacking ensembles did NOT provide a
    measurable benefit over the max-margin decision boundary established by LinearSVC.
  - MultinomialNB's conditional independence assumption degrades ensemble voting precision
    on nuanced vocabulary overlap (specifically Business/Finance vs Technology).
